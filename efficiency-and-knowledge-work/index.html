Efficiency and Knowledge Work

Efficiency is typically defined as input energy over output energy. That is, it's a ratio we can think of as a percentage that slowly approaches 100% as we get better at engineer: removing friction, avoiding energy loss to undesired means (e.g. heat).

Therefore in mechanical engineering we have a bound to what is the most efficient machine possible. The top metric of one (or 100%) is the theoretical bound and human engineering can work towards that, likely with diminishing returns but potentially with breakthroughs over the years and centuries.
