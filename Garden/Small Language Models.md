A Small Language Model (SLM) is a name that emerged for any Large Language Model (LLM) that's been reduced to about 1 billion parameters (the acceptable range seems to be 10 million to 10 billion).

With the concerns of LLMs being very capable, but at huge energy and hardware costs, a clear next frontier is models that we could actually deploy to consumer devices or embedded systems.
