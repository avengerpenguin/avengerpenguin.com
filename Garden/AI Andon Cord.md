What is the Andon cord in a world where AI agents are create and potentially shipping code?

Evidence is that engineers regularly halting the process when they find an issue actually speeds up delivery despite the intuitive idea that it would interrupt people a lot.

Ideas:

- Separate supervisor agent with power to halt the pipeline.
- Dedicated gate agents with access to observability pre-deployment to replace where we might have manual y/n gates. They can err on the side of halting and escalating to humans.
