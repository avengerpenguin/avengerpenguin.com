Self-Actualization is Communal

I've long known about Maslow's pyramid of needs but only learned much later in life the claim that he was talking about self-actualisation of a community.

In Indigenous Self-Actualization Is Communal – Kindred Magazine his Summer with the indigenous Blackfoot people and how that influenced the model. It then follows that the model is really describing people co-operating in social interest, not trying to realise their own needs alone.

This is an interesting assertion that feeds further into my observations we've been driven away from the idea that There is no such thing as the individual and our species is communal. That is, the individualistic ideology pushed on us only allowed us to see the pyramid of needs as something a person strives for, not one a community can strive for.
